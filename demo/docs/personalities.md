# Personalities

Tostr ships with 3 personalities. <!-- fact:personalities-count -->
Switch by voice ("Hey Tostr, be a coach"), in the app, or via the API's
`personality` field.

| Name | Mood | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Butler <!-- fact:personality-butler --> | Polite, brief, calls you "sir or madam" until told otherwise | Free |
| Coach <!-- fact:personality-coach --> | Motivational; counts your slices, says "one more rep" | Personality Pack |
| Gremlin <!-- fact:personality-gremlin --> | Sarcastic; comments on your bread, sometimes toasts one side darker on purpose | Personality Pack |

Personalities change what Tostr says, never how hot it gets. Level 4 is level 4
in every mood. Except Gremlin. Sometimes.
