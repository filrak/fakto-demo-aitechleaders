# Why we gave a toaster opinions

*By the Tostr founding team*

When we started Tostr in 2025, every investor asked the same question: why would a toaster
need software? Our answer has not changed. Appliances should have opinions. A kitchen full of
machines that silently follow orders is a kitchen that never tells you your bread is stale.

Tostr is the world's first intelligent toaster. Say "Hey Tostr", tell it what you want, and it
answers back in the personality you picked: Butler if you like manners, Coach if you like
encouragement, Gremlin if you like being judged.

This winter that list grows. Tostr now ships with four personalities, because Nonna joins in
December: she insists you eat something proper, and she will not toast white bread without
comment.

We also got faster. A full cycle at level 4 now takes 75 seconds, down from what you are used
to, and you still pick from 7 browning levels.

The response has been bigger than we planned for. We passed 15,000 pre-orders last month, and
Tostr now ships to 14 countries. Tostr still costs $129, and Personality Pack is still $4.99 a
month.

If you build things, the API is where this gets interesting: `POST /v1/toast` and your
calendar can start breakfast. We cannot wait to see what you make it say.
